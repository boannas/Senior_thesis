$\text{TTD}_m$    & Normalized time-to-death of the mother                    & [0,1]   \\
$\text{TTD}_c$    & Normalized time-to-death of the child                     & [0,1]   \\
$I_{\text{total}}$& Cumulative injury accumulated by the mother over an episode & --    \\
$L_2$             & Euclidean (L2) distance between two genome weight vectors  & --      \\
$N_s$             & Number of stochastic rollouts per genome evaluation ($N_s = 10$) & --  \\
$N_{\text{seed}}$ & Number of independent evolution seeds ($N_{\text{seed}} = 30$)   & --  \\
$T$               & Episode length in simulation ticks ($T = 1000$)            & ticks   \\

$d_{\text{global}}$& Global deficit signal used by P1 plasticity regime        & --      \\
$d_k$             & Local deficit signal for action class $k$, used by P2      & --      \\
$\text{OT}$       & Oxytocin-like modulator signal (bonding/affiliation)        & a.u.    \\
$\text{CORT}$     & Cortisol-like modulator signal (stress/threat response)     & a.u.    \\
$P(a \mid s)$     & Conditional probability of selecting action $a$ given state $s$ & [0,1] \\
$w_{50}$          & Window size for rolling average of L2 genome drift (50 generations) & gen \\
